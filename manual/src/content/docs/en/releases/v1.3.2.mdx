## Changes

1. **Stable masked windows on the avatar-only sidebar**: `open --mask` now recognizes the supported avatar-only account trigger and masks its avatar and linked account-menu identity. Repeated injection preserves the sidebar control and callback while the private window becomes ready, preventing the observed sidebar flicker and readiness failure.
2. **Unblock Windows uninstall after an old Store package is removed**: Retire a pending update intent only after proving that its exact package generation is absent. Running packages, uncertain queries, and failed debugger removal still preserve the existing safety checks.

Upgrade: Run `inc update` to update the CLI and shared Runtime. This update does not patch or re-sign Codex.

Verification and limits: Both `open` and `open --mask`, sidebar hover/close, linked-menu masking, settings return, and session cleanup were verified on macOS; the masked-window fix and stale-package uninstall were also verified on Windows. The avatar-only sidebar keeps its original layout, and the temporary name appears in its linked menu. Full Settings continues to show the official account details. Automatic recovery after a real Windows Store update remains experimental; a real post-update first cold launch is still unverified.
